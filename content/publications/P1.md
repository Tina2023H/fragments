## Foreword
Current work on Partial differential equations are most focusing on numerical data. However, symbolic expressions are more valuable , in terms of explainable, and analyzable.

This work focus on training a transformer model with symbolic ODE expressions. By replacing absolute positional embedding with tree positional embedding, and employed contrastive learning to learn mathematical equivalent but syntactic different expression (i.e., x+1 and 1+x)
